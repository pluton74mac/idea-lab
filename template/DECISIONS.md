# Architectural Decision Records — <Idea Name>

Decisions are documented as they're made. Each ADR follows this format:

```markdown
## ADR #NNN — Title
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded

### Context
What is the issue? What constraints exist?

### Decision
What was decided?

### Rationale
Why this option? What alternatives were considered?

### Consequences
What are the implications?
```

---

*ADRs appear here as technical decisions are made.*
